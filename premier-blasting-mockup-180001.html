How It Works

Our Proven 4-Step Process

Every project follows our meticulous process, ensuring outstanding results with minimal disruption to your home or business.

1

Free Site Assessment

We visit your property, assess the surfaces, and provide a detailed, no-obligation quotation tailored to your specific requirements.

2

Pre-Work Preparation

We contain the work area by sheeting floors, sealing doorways, and protecting surrounding surfaces to ensure your property is fully safeguarded.

3

Precision Blasting

Using cutting-edge micro-blasting technology, we apply precision, low-pressure blasting to remove unwanted coatings and reveal the original surface.

4

Full Cleanup & Handover

We complete a thorough cleanup, removing all waste and debris, leaving your property immaculate and ready for the next stage of your project.
