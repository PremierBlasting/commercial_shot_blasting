Verified project details taken directly from the capability statement:

- Case Study 2 title: **ISS Property – Former Bakkavor Foods Facility**
- Location: **Wigan**
- Contract value: **£40,000**
- Duration: **150 hours**
- Problem summary: ISS Property required the structural steelwork at the former Bakkavor Foods facility to be prepared to an **SA 2.5 surface finish** before the application of an **intumescent fire protection coating**.
- Environmental challenge summary: sections of the steelwork were exposed to the elements, so weather conditions presented a significant challenge.
- Delivery summary: the client required a **complete one-stop solution**, with **shot blasting followed immediately by the application of a certified primer and topcoat** to meet fire protection specifications and prevent flash rusting.
- Programme summary: a comprehensive site survey and steel-section measurement were carried out to calculate the precise blasting time and develop an efficient project schedule.
- Delivery pace summary: through extended working hours and weekend shifts to make use of favourable weather, the project was completed in **10 days**, described as **five days ahead of programme**, without compromising quality or compliance.
