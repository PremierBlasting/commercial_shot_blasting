## Standard terminology source

The new standards guide must use limited, factual language and must not claim that every project achieves any particular grade. ISO’s published ISO 8501-1:2007 overview identifies it as a visual assessment standard for rust grades and preparation grades of steel surfaces, applicable to steel prepared for painting including blast-cleaning. It says grades are defined through written descriptions and representative photographs, and notes the standard is published but expected to be replaced by ISO/DIS 8501-1. Source: https://www.iso.org/standard/43426.html
