## Sitemap and crawler-heading checks

- Live `robots.txt` allows normal crawling, blocks only `/api/` and `/manus-storage/`, and declares `sitemap.xml` plus `sitemap-images.xml`.
- `sitemap.xml` is currently a single 910-URL `<urlset>`. The historical paths `/sitemap-main.xml`, `/sitemap-service-areas.xml`, and `/sitemap-counties.xml` each issue a 301 redirect to that same full sitemap. This is not a crawl block, but it does mean historic Search Console submissions no longer represent separate scoped sitemaps. A true sitemap index with distinct canonical child sitemaps is clearer for monitoring and diagnosing index coverage.
- Raw live HTML returns one `<h1>` each for the homepage, Structural Steel Frames, and Gloucester. DataForSEO’s rendered audit reports three H1s for those pages. Treat this as an issue to reproduce in a rendering-aware crawler or browser DOM before changing semantics; do not remove server-rendered H1s on the basis of the audit alone.
- Several DataForSEO ranking snippets retain legacy 07970 566409 / “within 24 hours” content for location pages, while current live HTML for representative Coventry, Crewe and Sheffield pages only exposed 07721 375756 and no deprecated response-time text. This is likely stale search-index text; request recrawls of the highest-value affected URLs rather than changing validated live copy.

## Official guidance used for recommendation framing

- Google confirms that redirects, `rel="canonical"`, and sitemap inclusion are stacking canonical signals, and that internal links should consistently point to the canonical URL: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/consolidate-duplicate-urls
- Google recommends concise, descriptive and distinct title text, with a clear main title and non-repetitive title boilerplate: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/title-link
- Google recommends people-first material with first-hand expertise, clear sourcing, and content that is not mass-produced merely to attract search traffic: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content

## Implications for prioritisation

1. Lift existing position-11–20 location winners before expanding more towns: Bristol, Peterborough, Derbyshire/Cornwall county clusters and selected city pages provide nearer-term leverage.
2. Build non-location commercial intent hubs for mobile/on-site blasting, blast cleaning, structural steel preparation, and intumescent-coating coordination; exclude hire, job and consumer DIY demand.
3. Earn relevant supplier, contractor, client and trade-association references to close the off-page authority gap. Preserve distinct brand roles between Commercial Shot Blasting and Premier Blasting; do not canonicalise one site to the other.
4. Tighten homepage title/description and reduce duplicated heading/DOM complexity while keeping crawler-visible content, canonical URLs and Site Visit routes intact.
